# title

What we've been doing since finishing Blog 1 was researching technologies (software and hardware) that already exist in our topic. I first searched up apps and websites not many people know about real estate, and one website I found about my topic was [HPD ONLINE: Find building information](https://hpdonline.nyc.gov/hpdonline/). This website allows people to look up information like safety conditions and property details for buildings across NYC. Another website/app I found was Openigloo. [The website](https://www.openigloo.com/) and app collects city inspection data and tenant reviews which are used to score buildings. It allows renters to check out buildings, landlords, read reviews and browse and apply for apartments.
